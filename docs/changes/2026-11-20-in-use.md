# LX-26 and SV-30 keep a tool someone is connected through

- The team packet: the boxes may be reached over SSH, VNC, telnet or RDP.  LX-26 (VNC, remote desktops, web terminals ...) and SV-30 (telnet, rsh) look for a live connection from another machine (ss or netstat; loopback ignored).  If there is one: IN USE NOW in red, and the default is keep / no (also with --batch, and in SV-30 even when named in --only).
